# Purchasing and supplier policy

- Every new supplier purchase order requires an explicit human YES or NO unless its total is at or below the configured approval threshold.
- Never create or submit an order unless the user explicitly asks for a draft or reorder.
- Respect each supplier's minimum order quantity. A draft can be larger than the requested quantity when needed to satisfy the MOQ.
- Use the supplier listed for the product in inventory. Do not infer substitutions or negotiate supplier terms.
- The application creates drafts only; approval records consent but does not transmit an order to a supplier.
